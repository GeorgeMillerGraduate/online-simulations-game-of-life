Supported rules & engines

Conway Life, Wireworld and binary B/S rules without B0. Both binary and four-state rules use cached quadtree evolution. Sparse mode is useful for small irregular patterns. Golly rules with other state systems or bounded topologies are excluded.

RLE, macrocell (.mc), Wireworld MCell (.mcl) and Life coordinate files are supported. Large patterns may need smaller jumps or more memory.
